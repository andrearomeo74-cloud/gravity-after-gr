# Gravity After General Relativity

This repository is a **conceptual, non-operational study** of gravity *after* General Relativity (GR).

The goal is **not** to falsify GR, replace it, or propose new physical entities, laws, or constants.

Instead, we explore a different framing:

> General Relativity is treated as a highly successful **regime-level closure description**,  
> not necessarily the final ontological account of gravitational dynamics.

---

## Motivation

Despite its extraordinary empirical success, General Relativity has resisted unification with quantum theory and has led to a century of tension around the **foundations of gravity**.

Rather than asking *“Is GR wrong?”*, this repository asks:

- What if GR is **exactly correct within its regime**?
- What if spacetime curvature is a **recording language**, not the substrate itself?
- What structural assumptions are baked into continuity-based descriptions?
- What remains invariant if gravity is reinterpreted beyond geometry?

---

## Scope and Constraints

This repository is intentionally bounded.

**Included:**
- Conceptual analysis
- Regime-based interpretation
- Structural and inferential framing
- Links to coherence, closure, and invariance

**Explicitly excluded:**
- New physical constants
- Executable models
- Empirical claims or predictions
- Quantum gravity proposals
- Claims of falsifying GR

---

## Relation to Other Work

This repository is consistent with:
- Regime-based congruity frameworks
- Bounded intelligence and closure analysis
- Previous conceptual studies on dark matter, MOND, and cosmology

It is designed to sit **downstream of GR**, not in opposition to it.
